Number Reading and Pole Residents

To read the number, the photo inside the frame is sent to a reading AI (Google Gemini). This game does not store your photos. Your consent to this explanation is confirmed at the start, and nothing is sent before you consent or during the indoor trial. Processing on Google's side is governed by Google's terms of use. For details, see How Photos and Numbers Are Handled.

If the number can be read, the character and rarity are fixed by the prefecture and the plate number. The distribution is R 70%, SR 25% and SSR 5%. As for characters, the Denki of that prefecture appears about 7–8% of the time, Denki from the same area are twice as likely to appear as those from other areas, and you can meet all 47 (poles you met in an earlier version keep the resident they had at that time). There is no guaranteed local SSR on your first summon; meeting a pole for the first time gives +5 affinity (max. 500). A reunion is not a new summon: you can receive +3 affinity once per day per pole, resetting every morning at 4:00 AM (the day you first meet counts as that day's one time). Only if the number cannot be read is the conventional random draw used. Numbers and meeting records are included in your save data and backups. Because of misreadings, identical numbers and the like, results may not exactly match the real pole.
